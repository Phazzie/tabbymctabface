## 🤔 Why $2.99 Instead of $3.99?

### Psychology
- **$2 vs $3**: Feels like "under $3" (magic threshold)
- **Impulse buy**: $2.99 doesn't require mental budgeting
- **Lower barrier**: More users convert → more lifetime value
- **Competitive**: OneTab is free, Workona is $8/mo, we're perfectly positioned

### Math
- $3.99/mo = $47.88/year
- $2.99/mo = $35.88/year
- **25% price drop, but likely 40-50% increase in conversions**

**Example**:
- $3.99: 1,000 users × 3% conversion = 30 users × $3.99 = **$120/mo**
- $2.99: 1,000 users × 5% conversion = 50 users × $2.99 = **$150/mo**

**25% more revenue with lower price** (better conversion rate).
