What it does

🍀

I'm Feeling Lucky

Closes one random tab. Pinned tabs and the one you're looking at are safe. Everything else is fair game.

📁

Quick tab groups

Pick a few tabs, give them a name, and they're grouped in one click. Tabby will have opinions about it.

💬

75 quips

Questions

Will it close a tab I need?

It might. That's the deal. It never closes pinned tabs or the tab you're on, and Chrome's own Ctrl+Shift+T (⌘+Shift+T on a Mac) brings a closed tab back.

Does it read my browsing?

Tabby looks at your open tabs' titles and addresses so it can count, group and close them, and pick the right quip. That stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. The privacy policy has the details.

Why does it need these permissions?

Tabs and tab groups to count, close and group tabs. Notifications to deliver quips. Storage to remember how many quips you've earned.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no upsell.
