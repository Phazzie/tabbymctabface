HOUSE RULES

TabbyMcTabface privacy policy · last updated November 27, 2026

TabbyMcTabface is a Chrome extension. This sheet says what it touches and what it does with it. Short version: everything stays in your browser, and nothing is collected, sent, sold or shared.

1. What I read

To count, close and group your tabs, the extension reads the title, address (URL), pinned state and group of your open tabs through Chrome's tabs API. It uses this only in the moment, to decide which tab to close, which tabs to group, and which note or easter egg fits.

2. What I write down

One number, in Chrome's local extension storage: how many notes (quips) it has left you. It does not store your tab titles, addresses or browsing history.

3. What I send

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts. The notes and easter eggs are bundled inside the extension.

4. Keys I asked for (permissions)

  • tabs: read open tabs and close the one picked by "I'm Feeling Lucky".
  • tabGroups: create and name tab groups.
  • notifications: show notes as Chrome notifications.
  • storage: remember the notes-delivered count.

5. Moving out

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

6. If the rules change

The new version goes on this page with a new date.

7. Complaints

Questions go to the project's GitHub issues page.
