Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

TabbyMcTabface is a Chrome extension. This page explains what it touches and what it does with it. The short version: everything stays in your browser, and nothing is collected, sent, sold or shared.

What the extension reads

To count, close and group your tabs, the extension reads the title, address (URL), pinned state and group of your open tabs through Chrome's tabs API. It uses this only in the moment, to decide which tab to close, which tabs to group, and which quip or easter egg fits.

What it stores

It keeps one number in Chrome's local extension storage: how many quips it has delivered. It does not store your tab titles, addresses or browsing history.

What it sends

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts. Quips and easter eggs are bundled inside the extension.

Permissions

  • tabs: read open tabs and close the one picked by "I'm Feeling Lucky".
  • tabGroups: create and name tab groups.
  • notifications: show quips as Chrome notifications.
  • storage: remember the quips-delivered count.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
